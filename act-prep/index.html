A simple rhythm

Three sessions, plus a practice block when scheduled

  1. Session 1Learn one idea; do a small, untimed set.
  2. Session 2Practice it under a short timer; enter every miss in the error log.
  3. Session 3Correct and explain the misses; decide what comes next.
  4. Practice weeksReserve one quiet block for the test, then another for scoring and review.
Keep score reports and reflections private. This page saves only completion checks in this browser. Keep raw scores, error logs, and any test-registration details in a private Google Doc, Sheet, or paper notebook. Back up the completion record once each week.

Private study tools

Turn each miss into a next step.

Error-log prompts
  1. What skill or question type was this?
  2. What did I do or think that led to the wrong answer?
  3. What rule, evidence, or procedure makes the correct answer work?
  4. Was the cause content, reading the question, timing, calculator use, or carelessness?
  5. What one small practice set will I do next?
Score-check routine

After every timed set, record: questions attempted, correct, unanswered, minutes used, the two most common error causes, and one specific next action. Do not spend the whole review session merely reading answer explanations—redo missed problems before looking.

What “done” means each week

Checking a lesson means the reading or video was completed, the practice was attempted honestly, and the score/error-log note was written. A low score is still useful data, not a reason to skip the review.
